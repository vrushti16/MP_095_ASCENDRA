========================================================================
ASCENDRA — BACKGROUND MUSIC AUDIO ASSET LOCATION
========================================================================

Place your background music audio files (e.g., .wav, .mp3, .ogg) in this folder:

  Assets/Audio/Music/

Recommended Naming Convention:
  - ASCENDRA_Exploration.wav
  - ASCENDRA_Puzzle.wav
  - ASCENDRA_Boss.wav
  - ASCENDRA_Victory.wav
  - ASCENDRA_MainMenu.wav

Unity Inspector Setup:
1. Open Unity Editor.
2. Select the GameObject with [BackgroundMusicManager] attached (or play the game so it auto-spawns).
3. Drag your audio clip from Assets/Audio/Music/ into the 'Exploration Clip' field in the Background Music Manager inspector component.
4. Adjust Volume, Fade In, and Fade Out settings as desired.
